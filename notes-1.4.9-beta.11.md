This beta fixes problems in 1.4.9-beta.10:

- Fix: STEINS;GATE failed to start from MeguLit. MeguLit now reads the launch arguments the game's launcher passes and starts the game directly; when it cannot read them it still hands the launch to Steam
- UI: small text is at least 12px, clearer labels, solid primary buttons, an outlined dictionary search box, collapsible word definitions and easier-to-see mastery controls
- Fix: words written only in kana now show their headword

Only tested locally by the developer; there may be problems not found yet, and fixes will keep coming
